Chocolate Feud

Wiring: Team Milk's Kiss on Left arrow, Team Dark's Kiss on Right arrow, and an Earth clip under each team's foil star (the Makey Makey has several Earth holes). No second Kiss? The Left and Right keys on the keyboard work as buzzers too.

Face-offRead the question. One student from each team, hand on their star. First to touch their Kiss gets to answer. If their answer isn't the top one, you can press S to give play to the other team.
1 to 8Flip that answer (check your key; close enough counts)
XStrike. Three strikes and the other team gets one try to steal.
BackspaceUndo a strike you gave by mistake
EnterShow the leftover answers, then go to the next question
