Wiring map

#FoodClip it toNote
Line the food up left to right in number order, so it goes low note to high note like a real piano. On the chopping board: cut the food first, pat it dry, wipe up any juice, and leave a finger's width between pieces. If two foods touch, or a wet streak of juice connects them, both notes play. You have one spare alligator clip: keep it ready for a food that stops working.

Teacher cheat sheet

Before the kids arrive

  1. Plug the Makey Makey into the computer. Open benfminer.github.io/chocolate-touch → Fruit Piano. Press ⛶ for full screen.
  2. Turn the volume up.
  3. Click once anywhere on the page. (A yellow "Click anywhere" banner means the page lost focus.)
  4. Put on the wrist strap. Press ⚙ and touch each food: the matching key should light up.
  5. In ⚙, tap Change fruit if a key's picture doesn't match your table. Press ⚙ again to finish.
  6. Park the mouse pointer in the empty space above the keys. The Click pad clicks wherever the pointer is.

Running it

Free Play (warm-up): everyone gets a quick turn. Try all three sounds. The Chocolate Trumpet gets laughs.

Learn a Song: start with Hot Cross Buns (it only uses apple, grapes, banana). Press Listen first. The next food bounces on screen. Wrong notes are fine; it waits. Then Mary Had a Little Lamb, Jingle Bells, Twinkle Twinkle. Twinkle is the only song that uses the carrot.

Copy Cat: one player wears the strap. Watch, then copy. One more note each round. A miss turns the food to chocolate. Enter = next player. The screen keeps "Best today."

Teacher keys

EnterStart / next player (Copy Cat). Start the song over (Learn a Song).
T (secret)Next food a kid touches comes out as a gloopy chocolate trumpet, then back to normal. Tiny dot in the bottom-right corner: solid = armed.
1–6Play the foods from the keyboard (handy for testing).
Tap the screenKids can also tap the food keys on the smart screen.

If something goes wrong

No food works at allStrap snug on bare skin? Strap clip on Earth? Click the page once. USB plugged in?
One food doesn't workPush the clip deeper into it. Cut a fresh piece. Swap in the spare clip.
Carrot is spottyCarrots are drier than fruit. Cut a fresh end and push the clip in deeper. Still bad? Use a spare piece of cucumber, apple or jicama and change key 6 in ⚙.
Two notes play at onceTwo foods are touching, or juice on the chopping board connects them. Spread them out and wipe the board dry.
Mode or sound changes by itselfThat's the Click pad clicking a button. Move the pointer to empty space.
No soundVolume up, then click the page once (browsers wait for one click before playing sound).
