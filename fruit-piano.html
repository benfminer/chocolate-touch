Fruit Piano

Wiring

  • Earth goes to the player. Easiest: clip a grounding wrist strap onto Earth and have the player wear it snug on bare skin (gray side in). That frees up all 7 alligator clips for food and a spare.
  • 6 clips go to the fruit: Left, Up, Right, Down, Space, Click. Line the fruit up in that order, low note to high note.
  • Press ⚙ to test each fruit and make the screen match your real fruit.
  • Click fruit: the Makey Makey clicks wherever the mouse pointer is. Park the pointer in an empty spot (like the middle of the screen), not on a button or the Home link.
  • If you have a jumper wire, the W pin on the back can be the 6th fruit instead of Click.

Modes

  • Free Play: just play. Try the Chocolate Trumpet.
  • Learn a Song: the next fruit bounces. Wrong notes are fine, it just waits for the right one.
  • Copy Cat: watch, then copy. One more note each round. Miss one and it turns to chocolate.

Teacher keys

  • Enter start / next player / start the song over
  • 1–6 play the fruits from the keyboard
  • T secret chocolate note: the next fruit a kid touches comes out as a gloopy chocolate trumpet, then it's back to normal. Tiny dot bottom-right: solid = armed.
  • Kids can also tap the fruit keys right on the smart screen.
